Campaign Journal

In-Game Dates: Ches 1

Players complete characters and their back stories which are integrated into the world, and learn about the world and recent events, including the Impilturian Earthquake on the 17th of Hammer, 1502 DR.

In-Game Dates: Ches 1 - 2

Ellawain, Ikari, and Delon attend the king's ball where they learn of rumors, uncover secrets, and compete in challenges. The king tasks the characters to to accompany the prince to West-Over-Farthering, where they will meet up with additional party members. On the journey, the party are attacked by brigands at the sword bridge and are assisted by Seraphina Vale in apprehending two of them. The heroes continue to the crossroads where they meet Captain Wardstone Thane, Nhalos the Moondog, and defeat a duo of Ankheg.

In-Game Dates: Ches 3 - 4

Prince Edric, Ellawain ,Ikari, and Delon reach West-Over-Farthing and go to the Broken Keel Inn/Tavern to meet up with an expected companion, Vichenzo Visconti, who had arrived the night before by magical means. They are also unexpectedly joined by the bard, Stiora Autumnblossom who agrees to travel with them and chronicle their "legendary exploits". After averting a possible problem between the prince and the barkeep, the heroes set forth.

Together, the companions reach the outskirts of West-Over-Farthing and are accosted by a band of marauding goblins at the Western Watchtower. Victorious, the adventurers head north along the chasm to locate the point at which Narmus Sting had fallen. They locate the position, and descend to the depths of the chasm on ropes, fighting off drooling climbers on the way. They locate Narmus Sting's body and an entrace to a mysterious underground chamber of worked stone.

In-Game Dates: Ches 4 - 5

The six companions enter a strange eldritch set of chambers accessed from the earthquake chasm. They venture through a series of five chambers which were guarded by red rune-covered guardians including corpse husks, living spells, and a protective warden, all while feeling eerily uncomfortable due to the otherworldly nature of the magical emanations.

The heroes reach the final chamber, and find Ultheera Windmere, Vichenzo's mentor, seemingly trapped by eldritch chains of mystical runes. They work to extract her from the prison of runes, yet when they successfully rescue her, she ages rapidly over the course of a minute, before dying of old age with a horrified expression on her face. The eldritch chains momentarily quiet, before assaulting Prince Edric, who flees the chamber, but not before becoming "infected" by the bands of runic energy. His life seemingly lies on the edge of a precipice.

In-Game Dates: Ches 5 - 7

The heroes race to save the life of Prince Edric. Ikari carries him on his back for the first leg of the journey, through the forest and across the earthquake chasm back to West-Over-Farthing. As they approached the village, it became clear that a host of goblins and hobgoblins had vile plans for raiding the village. Through clever magic, Stiora was able to charm their leaders, and allow for safe passage, while Ellawain engineered a schism between the goblins and hobgoblins through a shrewd payoff and their inherent greed. Delon disappeared into the woods. The heroes head to the stables, acquire their horses and additional spare horses, and set forth at top speed for Lyrabar.

As the heroes race through the night toward Lyrabar, they ditch the exhausted horses, and mount the remaining fresh horses. Ikrai wisely blasts notes on the hunting horn that had been given to him by the captain of the patrol. Delon reappears with a final set of fresh horses, which he had apparantly recieved from the patrol. With dawn brings a threat from above, as a host of vile manticore begin hunting thr heroes and their mounts. Several manticores satiate their hunger on the corpses of fallen horses, while two follow Delon as he makes a break from the group to draw them away, leaving only a single beast following, demanding man flesh. The heroes fend it off with well placed attacks and spell damage, and have a clear path for Lyrabar.

The heroes reach Lyrabar at midday, and usher the fading prince into the infirmary. As the prince is attended by a host of healers, the king arrives and rushes to his side. The prince succumbs to the curse, and his body is blasted apart, spraying entrails and rune-chains in all directions. Nearby attendants and the king are likewise afflicted by the phage, and a woman had been shunted out the window by panicked onlookers. From the broken body of the prince rises an otherworldly fiend, who immediately assails and kills the king, which in turn ushers in another fiend. The heroes defeat a total of four fiends, and all attendants receive magical curse removal after the incident from the court wizard Peverill Thrice-Bound. The second calamity has come - the king and his named heirs are dead.
